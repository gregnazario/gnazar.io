Following money on-chain means staring at raw transfer lists. aptos-tracker
syncs fungible-asset activity from the Aptos Indexer GraphQL API into local
SQLite and renders it as interactive Sankey diagrams and force-directed
graphs built on D3.

Heuristics handle the messy parts automatically — detecting account
boundaries like DEX pools, exchanges, and contracts; categorizing flows by
entry-function patterns for tax reporting; resolving `.apt` names and labeling
well-known addresses from a built-in registry.
