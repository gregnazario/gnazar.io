Move code samples deserve real highlighting. highlightjs-move is an
npm-published Highlight.js grammar covering modern Move 2.x — enums, pattern
matching, lambdas, signed integers up to i256, attributes — alongside hex and
byte-string literals and the Move Specification Language.

It registers under `move` with `aptos-move` aliases, ships ESM and CommonJS
builds plus CDN bundles, and keeps a dedicated subpath for Highlight.js v10
projects.
