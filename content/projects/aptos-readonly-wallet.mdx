An Aptos wallet that answers a deceptively useful question: what would
happen if this account did that? It injects as a standard wallet into any
dapp, but instead of signing transactions with real keys, it simulates them
as the target account and shows the resulting state.

Handy for auditing what a dapp would do to an account, debugging
transactions, and exploring protocol behavior without risking funds.
