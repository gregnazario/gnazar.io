A self-hostable Multi-ed25519 multisig backend and UI for Aptos, in the
spirit of Msafe. It lets a group of keys collectively manage an account —
proposing, approving, and executing transactions — without handing custody
to a hosted service.

Running it yourself means the coordination layer is under your control,
which is the point of multisig in the first place.
