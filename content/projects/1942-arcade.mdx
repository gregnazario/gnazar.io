My Carnegie Mellon 18-545 capstone: the classic vertical shooter 1942,
reimplemented in Verilog and running on an FPGA. Sprites, scrolling,
collision, and game logic all in RTL — a reminder that games used to be
hardware.

An oldie but a favorite: the project that got me hooked on building things
close to the metal.
